# Research Domains

## 1. Water Resources & Hydrological Modeling
**One-line:** Physically based and data-driven modeling of river-basin hydrology, water balances, and streamflow across India's climates.

**Expanded:** Two decades of peer-reviewed work — watershed water-balance modeling, curve-number streamflow uncertainty reduction, karstic spring prediction via support vector machines — spanning Himalayan, peninsular, and eastern Indian basins. Forms the methodological backbone underpinning nearly every other domain below.

**Visual direction:** Topographic river-basin map with flow-accumulation overlay; blue-gradient watershed heatmap.

**Keywords:** Watershed Modeling · Streamflow Prediction · Water Balance · Machine Learning Hydrology · Curve Number Method

**Priority:** Flagship

---

## 2. Himalayan Cryosphere & Glacial Hazards
**One-line:** Assessing glacial lake outburst flood hazard, snowmelt dynamics, and downstream risk across the Himalayan cryosphere.

**Expanded:** Anchored by the *Water Resources Research* study on GLOF hazard and downstream risk, plus two active DST-funded projects modeling snowmelt and glacier-melt runoff in the Sikkim and Teesta catchments. Complemented by CMIP5/CMIP6-based precipitation and temperature extreme-trend analyses across Himalayan states.

**Visual direction:** Satellite imagery of a glacial lake with hazard-zone contour overlay.

**Keywords:** Glacial Lake Outburst Floods · Snowmelt Modeling · Himalayan Hydrology · CMIP5/CMIP6 Downscaling · Cryosphere Risk

**Priority:** Flagship

---

## 3. Climate Extremes, Drought & Ecosystem Resilience
**One-line:** Quantifying drought severity, vegetation stress, and ecosystem resilience to hydroclimatic extremes nationwide.

**Expanded:** Copula-based drought characterization, probabilistic vegetation drought-likelihood mapping, and two *Scientific Reports* papers on ecosystem resilience to hydroclimatic disturbance. District-level ecohydrological resilience assessments translate statistical climate extremes into on-the-ground agricultural and ecological consequences.

**Visual direction:** India drought-severity choropleth with NDVI vegetation-stress overlay.

**Keywords:** Drought Characterization · Copula Statistics · Ecosystem Resilience · Vegetation Stress · Hydroclimatic Extremes

**Priority:** Core

---

## 4. Atmospheric Rivers, Aerosols & Air Quality
**One-line:** Characterizing atmospheric rivers and aerosol transport through predictive, AI-driven air-quality forecasting.

**Expanded:** Two 2023 Springer volumes on aerosol atmospheric rivers, an operational Air Quality Prediction System, and active doctoral work on atmospheric-river predictability and black-carbon aerosol forecasting. A 2024-filed patent for an Atmospheric Rivers Prediction System signals this as the lab's newest growth area.

**Visual direction:** Satellite plume visualization of an atmospheric river; AQI heatmap over Indian cities.

**Keywords:** Atmospheric Rivers · Aerosol Transport · Air Quality Forecasting · Black Carbon · Predictive Climatology

**Priority:** Emerging

---

## 5. Wetlands & Water Quality Remote Sensing
**One-line:** Monitoring Ramsar wetland health and water quality using satellite remote sensing and AI.

**Expanded:** A dedicated 2024 volume on monitoring India's Ramsar wetlands, an operational Wetlands Analysis Platform, and a granted patent (No. 576312) for wetland water-quality monitoring. Extended by undergraduate-level water-quality threat assessments for specific Ramsar sites, combining remote sensing with field validation.

**Visual direction:** Wetland satellite imagery with a water-quality index color overlay.

**Keywords:** Wetland Monitoring · Ramsar Sites · Water Quality AI · Remote Sensing · Ecosystem Health

**Priority:** Core

---

## 6. AI-Driven Water Governance & Smart Cities
**One-line:** Translating hydro-climate research into deployable AI decision-support tools and smart-city water infrastructure.

**Expanded:** The Smart India Flood Analysis Tool, Central India Water Portal, and Central India Water Body Analysis platform convert the lab's science into public-facing infrastructure. Reinforced by two 2025-filed patents (flood and wetland risk assessment systems) and doctoral research on net-zero smart urban planning.

**Visual direction:** Smart-city network overlay fused with a flood-risk map and dashboard UI elements.

**Keywords:** Decision Support Systems · Flood Risk AI · Smart Cities · GIS Portals · Water Governance

**Priority:** Flagship

## Best 3 Flagship Domains for Homepage Emphasis

1. **Water Resources & Hydrological Modeling** — the deepest, longest-running publication record; it's the scientific identity everything else is built on.
2. **AI-Driven Water Governance & Smart Cities** — the most visually compelling and differentiated: patents, live tools, and translational impact resonate with funders and international collaborators, not just academic peers.
