## Research Domains

Six domains, two decades of hydro-climate science — from Himalayan cryosphere risk to deployable AI systems — unified by one mission: turning data into public resilience.

### Water Resources & Hydrological Modeling
Physically based, data-driven modeling of river-basin hydrology and streamflow across India.

Two decades of peer-reviewed research — water-balance modeling, streamflow uncertainty reduction, karstic spring prediction — spanning Himalayan, peninsular, and eastern Indian river basins. The methodological foundation for every domain below.

**Keywords:** Watershed Modeling · Streamflow Prediction · Water Balance · Machine Learning Hydrology

### Himalayan Cryosphere & Glacial Hazards
Assessing glacial lake outburst flood hazard and snowmelt dynamics across the Himalayas.

Anchored by landmark GLOF hazard research and two DST-funded projects modeling snowmelt and glacier-melt runoff in Sikkim and Teesta. Extended through CMIP5/CMIP6 climate-extreme trend analysis across Himalayan states.

**Keywords:** Glacial Lake Outburst Floods · Snowmelt Modeling · Himalayan Hydrology · Cryosphere Risk

### Climate Extremes, Drought & Ecosystem Resilience
Quantifying drought severity and ecosystem resilience to hydroclimatic extremes nationwide.

Copula-based drought characterization and vegetation drought-likelihood mapping, backed by two Scientific Reports studies on ecosystem resilience. District-level assessments translate statistical extremes into agricultural and ecological consequences nationwide.

**Keywords:** Drought Characterization · Ecosystem Resilience · Vegetation Stress · Hydroclimatic Extremes

### Atmospheric Rivers, Aerosols & Air Quality
Characterizing atmospheric rivers and aerosol transport through AI-driven air-quality forecasting.

Two Springer volumes on aerosol atmospheric rivers, an operational forecasting system, and active doctoral research on predictability and black-carbon aerosols — the lab's fastest-growing frontier, reinforced by a 2024 patent filing.

**Keywords:** Atmospheric Rivers · Aerosol Transport · Air Quality Forecasting · Black Carbon

### Wetlands & Water Quality Remote Sensing
Monitoring Ramsar wetland health and water quality using satellite remote sensing.

A dedicated volume on India's Ramsar wetlands, an operational analysis platform, and a granted patent for water-quality monitoring — extended by field-validated water-quality threat assessments for specific wetland sites.

**Keywords:** Wetland Monitoring · Ramsar Sites · Water Quality AI · Remote Sensing

### AI-Driven Water Systems & Decision Intelligence
Translating hydro-climate research into deployable AI decision-support tools and smart-city systems.

A national flood tool, a regional water portal, and three 2024–2025 patent filings convert hydro-climate science into public infrastructure — reinforced by doctoral research on net-zero smart urban planning.

**Keywords:** Decision Support Systems · Flood Risk AI · Smart Cities · GIS Portals

## Projects & Applications

Research translates into deployed systems: national-scale platforms, operational dashboards, and patented technologies that convert two decades of hydro-climate science into public infrastructure.

### Featured Flagship Projects

**Smart India Flood Analysis Tool**
AI-powered flood analysis platform delivering rapid, remote-sensing-based insight for national disaster response teams.
Analyzes flood events across Indian regions using satellite data and AI, enabling rapid extent assessment for disaster response. Its methodology underpins the 2025-filed Flood Risk Assessment System patent.

**Central India Water Portal**
Centralized regional water-data portal serving researchers, planners, and policymakers across Central India.
Consolidates water-resource data for Central India into a single access point, reducing duplication across studies and giving planners a unified, authoritative reference for regional water-security decisions.

**Wetlands Analysis Platform**
Satellite-driven wetland mapping platform assessing ecosystem extent and condition across India.
Maps and assesses wetland extent and ecological condition, directly supporting the Ramsar wetlands research program and the lab's granted water-quality monitoring patent, its first deployed intellectual property.

### Operational Research Platforms

**Central India Water Body Analysis**
Continuous dashboard tracking surface water body extent and change across Central India.
Feeds long-term monitoring of surface water resources across the region, generating time-series evidence that directly informs drought-response planning and regional water-security assessments for policymakers.

**Air Quality Prediction System**
AI-based forecasting system translating aerosol and pollution research into daily predictions.
Operationalizes the lab's aerosol and air-pollution science into a usable forecasting service, giving the public and policymakers timely, data-driven visibility into air-quality conditions and emerging pollution risk.

### Intellectual Property & Deployment

**Wetland Water Quality Monitoring System** — *Patent No. 576312, Granted*
Granted patent for a novel wetland water-quality monitoring method and system.
The lab's only granted patent to date validates its wetlands remote-sensing research as deployable, protected technology — a benchmark for the IP pipeline that follows.

**Flood Risk Assessment System** — *Patent Filed, 2025*
Patent-pending system formalizing flood-risk assessment methodology for institutional and commercial deployment.
Filed in 2025, this patent formalizes the Smart India Flood Analysis Tool's methodology, positioning the lab's flood-risk science for institutional licensing and broader commercial deployment.

**Wetland Risk Assessment System** — *Patent Filed, 2025*
Patent-pending system extending wetland monitoring into a broader ecological risk-assessment framework.
Filed in 2025, this patent complements the granted water-quality monitoring system with a broader risk-assessment layer, strengthening the lab's wetlands intellectual-property portfolio ahead of deployment.

**Atmospheric Rivers Prediction System** — *Patent Filed, 2024*
Patent-pending AI system predicting atmospheric river formation, timing, and downstream impact.
Filed in 2024, this patent is the intellectual-property counterpart to the lab's atmospheric-rivers publication line and doctoral research, anchoring its fastest-growing research domain in protected technology.
